Menu bar for macOS

Sketch over any window.

ExcaliCursor draws on top of the screen with Excalidraw’s sketch strokes. Press a hotkey, mark up the window you are in, and leave that app in focus.

Draw on the window you already have

The overlay stays out of the way until a hotkey wakes it. Your marks remain until you hide or clear them.

  • On-demand hotkey

    It stays quiet until you press a hotkey, such as ⌘⇧D, ⌘D, or a double-tap of ⌥.

  • Clicks pass through

    When drawing is off, the overlay ignores the pointer. Clicks, scrolls and keystrokes go to the app underneath.

  • The dock follows the edge

    Drag the toolbar by its grip. Near a side it turns vertical. Near the top or bottom it turns horizontal. Double-click the grip to snap back to the top center.

  • Laser pointer

    A crimson trail with a soft cursor glow, for screen shares, code walkthroughs and presentations. Press K.

  • Stroke, sloppiness and theme

    Pick a stroke color and a width: Thin, Bold or Extra. Rough.js sloppiness runs from Architect to Artist to Cartoon. Fill with hachure, and switch the toolbar between dark and light.

  • Copy a PNG

    Press ⌘C to put a transparent PNG on the clipboard. Launch at login uses SMAppService. If registration fails, the toggle reverts and Settings shows the error.

Install

You need macOS 13 or later, and Swift 5.9 or the Xcode Command Line Tools.

  1. 1

    Build the app

    Compiles ExcaliCursor.app.

    make app
  2. 2

    Launch ExcaliCursor

    The sketch icon appears in the menu bar.

    make run
  3. 3

    Build a disk image

    Writes build/ExcaliCursor.dmg, with a shortcut to /Applications.

    make dmg

Turn it on

The default hotkey is ⌘⇧D. Settings also offers ⌘D and ⌥D, or a double-tap of ⌥. The menu bar icon shows the shortcut you saved.

Double-tap ⌥ and Accessibility

The keyboard shortcut works without extra permission. Double-tap ⌥ needs ExcaliCursor allowed in Accessibility settings. If that monitor is unavailable, Settings shows a way to open them. Open Accessibility Settings.
